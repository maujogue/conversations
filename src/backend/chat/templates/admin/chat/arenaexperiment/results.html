Win rate is the share of decisive votes (a side was picked) where the challenger's answer was preferred; "both good" and "both bad" votes are counted separately. Results remain indicative until there are at least {{ h.threshold }} decisive votes and the 95% interval excludes 50%. Reaching the sample threshold alone does not establish a preference. Cost ratio is champion cost divided by challenger cost on the same turns, from the prices of the LLM configuration frozen on each comparison ({{ h.unpriced_answers }} answers have no recorded price). Second opinions asked for by users are counted apart and never enter the sampled win rate. Verdict: promote when the interval is entirely above 50%, promote_for_footprint when it still includes 50% but the challenger is at least 30% lighter in Wh per answer and no slower at p95, keep otherwise, indicative under {{ h.threshold }} decisive votes. Promotion writes the model into the tier settings and is logged in the routing tier history.
